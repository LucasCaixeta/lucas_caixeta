# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
- Recruiters and hiring managers who land on the site from LinkedIn, a referral or a search, usually skimming several candidates, deciding in seconds whether this person is worth a message.
- Engineering leaders and peers checking who Lucas is before or after a conversation.

## Product Purpose
Personal site of Lucas Caixeta, technical lead and front-end engineer in Lisbon. Passive presence: Lucas is not actively job hunting, but wants good opportunities to reach him. Success = a qualified visitor understands who he is, trusts the profile and writes to him (email or LinkedIn) without friction.

## Positioning
13 years in product teams on the web; today technical lead of a large consumer platform at Philip Morris International; background in design systems (Cazoo, Tamanna, PMI), micro-frontends and team practice (Coding Dojo). Bilingual EN/PT, based in Lisbon.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static site: plain HTML + one CSS file, no framework, GitHub Pages deploy on push to `main` (`.github/workflows/deploy.yml`). Two pages: `index.html` (EN) and `pt/index.html` (PT); keep them in sync.
- Light/dark theme with manual toggle, EN/PT switch, print stylesheet, JSON-LD and hreflang must keep working.
- Every change is validated locally by Lucas before any commit/push.

## Brand Commitments
- Name, LC monogram (L + C strokes) and domain lucascaixeta.com.
- Voice: plain, calm, first person, no hype.
- Work is always credited to the team: "I was part of the team that built…", "we built", with Lucas's role at each company. Never imply solo authorship of team products.

## Evidence on Hand
- The experience entries (company, role, dates, description, stack) are the proof of work. Profile facts are owned by Lucas and must not be changed without his request.
- No case studies, screenshots, internal metrics or diagrams: past work was at private companies. Do not fabricate or add them.
- No downloadable CV on the site.
- Medium article on Coding Dojo; GitHub, LinkedIn, X profiles.
- React experience: 6+ years (not 10+).

## Product Principles
1. Passive, not pushy: easy contact, no "available now" or sales pressure.
2. Experience is the proof: make the roles, teams and outcomes easy to scan instead of adding new content.
3. Honest credit: team-first language everywhere.
4. Readable in seconds for a skimming recruiter, rewarding for a peer who reads everything.
5. Fast, accessible, bilingual by default.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA: contrast, keyboard focus, reduced motion, semantic landmarks and headings; EN and PT parity.
